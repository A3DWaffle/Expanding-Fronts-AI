# File Guide

This guide explains the general purpose of major AI script files in this repository.

## Core Files

### `constants.per`

Shared constants, identifiers, and values used by other AI script files.

### `combat-arm.per`

Logic related to combat branch choices, military behavior, and unit direction.

### `military-population.per`

Standard military population logic and unit composition support.

### `military-population-hard.per`

Hard difficulty military population logic and unit composition support.

### `research.per`

Research and technology rules.

### `sn-gather.per`

Strategic number and resource gathering behavior.

### `attack.per`

Attack timing, military pressure, and related behavior.

### `warboat.per`

Naval behavior and warboat-related logic.

### `wonder-rush.per` and `wonder-kill.per`

Wonder-related AI behavior.

## Notes

The exact role of each file may change as the AI is reorganized. This file is meant to give visitors a general idea of what they are looking at when browsing the repository.
